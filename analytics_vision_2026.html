Marketing Performance Insights & Analytics

6-Month Foundation Roadmap | "Insure Your Unsure" Campaign Era

Mission: Transform Marketing into a Data-Driven Growth Engine

Build a comprehensive marketing analytics infrastructure that empowers Hollard's marketing team to measure, optimize, and scale the "Insure Your Unsure" campaign and all future initiatives. Move from gut-feel decisions to data-backed strategies that drive customer acquisition, retention, and brand growth across all channels.

Key Opportunity: New Brand Campaign Launch

The "Insure Your Unsure" campaign (launched Sept 2024) presents a unique opportunity to establish marketing analytics from day one, tracking performance, attribution, and ROI across all touchpoints as Hollard expands across 10+ African markets.

Hollard Marketing Context

  • New CMO Leadership: Hazel Chimhandamba driving brand repositioning
  • Campaign Focus: "Insure Your Unsure" - positive, human-centered messaging
  • Multi-Channel Mix: Digital ads, TV/radio, broker partnerships, retail activations
  • Pan-African Growth: 10+ markets requiring localized tracking
  • Partnership Model: Heavy focus on intermediaries, brokers, retailers

Current State Challenges

  • No Unified View: Campaign performance scattered across platforms
  • Attribution Gaps: Unable to connect ad spend to policy sales
  • Manual Reporting: Weekly reports take 10+ hours to compile
  • Inconsistent Metrics: Teams use different definitions for success
  • Limited Customer Insights: NPS and feedback not integrated with behavior

Attribution Framework

Multi-Touch Attribution Model

Track the full customer journey from first touch to policy purchase

Google Ad → Landing Page → Email → Broker Call → Policy Sale
Attribution Rules
  • • First Touch: 30% credit
  • • Last Touch: 40% credit
  • • Middle touches: 30% distributed

UTM Tagging Standards

Campaign Naming Convention:
[Region]_[Product]_[Channel]_[Campaign]_[Date]
Example: ZA_Motor_Meta_InsureUnsure_Q1-2026
UTM Parameters:
  • • utm_source: google, meta, tiktok
  • • utm_medium: cpc, social, email
  • • utm_campaign: [campaign name]
  • • utm_content: [ad variation]

Core Dashboard Suite

1. Executive Marketing Overview

CMO / LEADERSHIP

High-level view of marketing performance for strategic decision-making

Campaign ROI: "Insure Your Unsure" overall performance
Budget Health: Spend vs plan, pacing alerts
Customer Acquisition: Leads, conversions, CPA
Brand Metrics: NPS, awareness, sentiment
Refresh Rate
Daily (mornings)

2. Paid Media Performance

PERFORMANCE MARKETERS

Unified view of Google, Meta, TikTok and other paid channels

Cross-Channel Metrics: Impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions
Platform Breakdown: Google vs Meta vs TikTok performance
ROAS Analysis: Return on ad spend by campaign
Optimization Insights: Budget allocation recommendations
Refresh Rate
Real-time (API sync every hour)

3. Customer Journey Analytics

CX / INSIGHTS

Track how customers move from awareness to purchase

Multi-Touch Attribution: Full path to conversion
Funnel Analysis: Drop-off points and bottlenecks
Time to Convert: Days from first touch to sale
Touchpoint Analysis: Most effective channel combinations
Refresh Rate
Daily (evenings)

4. Campaign Deep Dive

BRAND / CAMPAIGNS

Detailed performance of "Insure Your Unsure" and all campaigns

Campaign Comparison: Performance across all active campaigns
Creative Performance: Which ad variations win
Geographic Breakdown: Performance by region/market
Trend Analysis: Performance over time (daily, weekly, monthly)
Refresh Rate
Daily (mornings)

5. Customer Insights & NPS

CX / RESEARCH

Voice of customer data integrated with behavior

NPS Tracking: Score trends, promoters vs detractors
Feedback Analysis: Themes from surveys and reviews
Segment Insights: NPS by customer type, product, channel
Behavior Correlation: NPS vs engagement, retention
Refresh Rate
Weekly

6. Marketing Budget Tracker

FINANCE / PLANNING

Real-time budget monitoring and forecasting

Budget Allocation: Planned vs actual by channel/campaign
Pacing Alerts: Over/under spend warnings
Cost Efficiency: CPL, CPA, CAC trends
Forecast: Projected spend to month/quarter end
Refresh Rate
Real-time (API sync every 2 hours)
